Quantum Research Groups

Profiles of research groups behind the quantum courses: what they work on, who is in them, what they publish, their projects and funding, news, events, open positions, and the courses their members teach. This is a pilot with one group per university.

Sources: each group's own website and its unit in the university's research portal (DTU Orbit, KU research portal, AU Pure, SDU Find Researcher, AAU VBN). Members, positions, projects and recent work were harvested on . News, events, grants and positions are fetched automatically from the groups' websites every Monday (last run ); sources that cannot be read automatically are marked in each panel.

The summaries and CFQT tags are written by Danish Quantum Academy and are not the groups' own texts; follow the links for their own descriptions. A member's line shows their current project in the portal, or else their most recent research output. Course links show courses taught by members of the group.
