Danish Quantum Education Map

Which parts of the European Competence Framework for Quantum Technologies (CFQT) do Danish university courses cover, and how far do they take students?

How to read this page

Coverage matrix. Each cell counts the courses that cover a CFQT subdomain, as primary or secondary content. Empty cells point to gaps in the educational offer. Select a cell to list those courses.

Proficiency levels. The estimated level a course brings students to in each proficiency area, from A1 (awareness) to C2. Courses on enabling technologies such as classical optics or nanofabrication often have no QT-specific proficiency and are counted under "none".

Course list. Courses are grouped by the CFQT domain of their main topic. Open a domain to see its courses, or use the filters and search; matching domains open by themselves. In each row, 4.4 filled is the course's primary subdomain and 2.2 outlined are further subdomains; I B2 is the estimated proficiency level per area. The small bars show how often quantum terms (dark) and related technical terms (grey) occur in the course description.

Status. All tags are first estimates from the course descriptions and will be checked with the course responsibles through the European Quantum Academy survey. Method and sources: About the data.
