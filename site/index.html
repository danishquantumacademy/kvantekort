Danish Quantum Education Map

How far do quantum courses at Danish universities take students, and which parts of the European Competence Framework for Quantum Technologies (CFQT) do they cover?

How to read this page

Choose a university or STX/HTX. The small triangles show Denmark, STX/HTX and each university (DTU, KU, AU, SDU, AAU). STX/HTX shows physics A at upper secondary school: the content that applies to all 144 stx and 34 htx schools, including the Kvant21 material on quantum physics and quantum technology. It is also counted in Denmark, together with the universities. Select one to update everything below.

Research of the educators. The third triangle in each card shows the research of the educators at that university: each educator counts once in each proficiency area, at their level there (C2 Innovation with at least three research outputs or supervised theses with their main topic in the area, C1 Specialisation with one or two, B2 Investigation with one that touches it). Select it to list the educators; each links to their row on the Educators page.

PhD and postdoc projects. Each university card has a second, smaller triangle below the courses: the PhD and postdoc projects on the CFQT content map, at research level (C2 in the area of the main topic, C1 in other areas it touches). Select it to see the projects in the big triangle and in the list, and a table of all positions under Selection. Supervised PhD projects are only available from DTU's research portal; for the other universities the projects of the five research groups are counted.

The triangle. The CFQT proficiency triangle has (I) Quantum concepts at the top, (II) QT hardware & software engineering on the left and (III) QT applications & strategies on the right. Levels grow from A1 in the centre to C2 at the corners. Each band shows the number of courses whose estimated level in that area is exactly that level; the shade tells how many, in five fixed steps, so the same shade means the same count for every university. Select a band to list its courses. The bars under the triangle show the same numbers per area, including courses without QT-specific proficiency ("none").

Content map and coverage. The tiles count courses covering each CFQT domain, as primary or secondary subdomain. Select a tile to list its courses, or unfold the coverage table to see each subdomain per university; empty cells point to gaps in the educational offer.

Course list. Courses are grouped by the CFQT domain of their main topic. In each row, 4.4 filled is the primary subdomain, 2.2 outlined are further subdomains, and I B2 is the estimated proficiency level per area.

Status. All tags and levels are first estimates from the course descriptions and will be checked with the course responsibles. Method and sources: About the data.
