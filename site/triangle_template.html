Danish Quantum Proficiency Triangle

The CFQT proficiency triangle shows how far courses take students in the three proficiency areas of the European Competence Framework for Quantum Technologies: (I) Quantum concepts at the top, (II) QT hardware & software engineering on the left, (III) QT applications & strategies on the right. Levels grow from A1 in the centre to C2 at the corners. Choose a university to see where its courses land. All levels are first estimates, to be validated with course responsibles.
