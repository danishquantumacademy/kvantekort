Learn quantum technology

Three areas that enabling quantum technologies   

Computing

Quantum computers

An ordinary computer works with bits that are either 0 or 1. A quantum computer uses qubits, which can be in a combination of 0 and 1 and can be linked to each other. For some problems, such as simulating molecules, this can make calculations possible that ordinary computers cannot manage.

Today's quantum computers are still small and sensitive to noise.

Sensing

Quantum sensors

Quantum states are extremely sensitive to their surroundings. Quantum sensors turn that sensitivity into very precise measurements of magnetic fields, time, gravity or temperature.

Examples: atomic clocks and magnetic sensors for medicine.

Communication

Quantum communication

Information carried by single particles of light cannot be copied without leaving a trace. That makes it possible to share encryption keys whose security rests on the laws of physics.

It complements new classical encryption that is safe against quantum computers.
