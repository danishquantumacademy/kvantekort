Teachers

Sources: the teachers named in the 2026/27 course catalogues, and their profiles in the universities' research portals (DTU Orbit, University of Copenhagen research portal, AU Pure, SDU Find Researcher, AAU VBN), harvested November 2026. Positions and organisational units are as stated in the portals. Fingerprint concepts are generated automatically by the portals and can contain odd terms. The research focus and its CFQT tags are written by Danish Quantum Academy from that portal data.

The fit notes compare the CFQT subdomains of each course with those of the teacher's research. They describe overlap in subject, not teaching quality, and many excellent courses are taught from a neighbouring field. All tags are first estimates and will be checked with the teachers through the European Quantum Academy survey.
