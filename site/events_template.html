Quantum events in Denmark

Conferences, workshops, summer schools, seminars, public talks and industry meetings on quantum science and technology in Denmark – from the universities, the research groups and the Danish quantum ecosystem.

How to read this page

Upcoming shows events from today on, the nearest first; Past shows earlier events, the newest first. Filter by type, audience or city, or search in titles, organisers and places.

Each event links to its own page, where the organiser gives the programme and registration. Add to calendar downloads a calendar file (.ics) you can open in Outlook, Google Calendar or Apple Calendar.

Events are collected from public pages only. Events without announced dates are shown at the start of their month with "dates to be announced". The list is not complete: tell Danish Quantum Academy about events that are missing.
