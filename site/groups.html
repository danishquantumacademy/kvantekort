Quantum Research Groups

The research groups behind the quantum courses: what they work on, who is in them, and what is happening now. A pilot with one group per university.

How to read this page

Cards and profile. Select a group card to open its profile below: summary and CFQT tags, news, grants and prizes, events, open positions, latest publications, projects, all members, and the courses taught by members.

Texts. The summaries and CFQT tags are written by Danish Quantum Academy and are not the groups' own texts; follow the links for their own descriptions. A member's line shows their current project in the research portal, or else their most recent research output.

Updates. Members, projects and recent work were harvested on . News, events, grants and positions are fetched automatically from the groups' websites every Monday (last run ); sources that cannot be read automatically are marked in each panel. Method and sources: About the data.
