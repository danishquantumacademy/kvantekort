The Danish Quantum Industry

Companies, facilities, investors and support organisations in the Danish quantum ecosystem, what their work covers on the CFQT content map, and what quantum job ads ask for.

How to read this page

Organisations. The list is the Danish Quantum Ecosystem Map of the Danish Business Authority and the Danish Quantum Community (updated 11 November 2025). Danish Quantum Academy wrote a short summary of each, its role in the value chain and the CFQT subdomains of its work, and checked the companies in the Central Business Register (CVR) for start date and place.

What the companies do. A company that develops quantum technology is placed at C2 Innovation in the proficiency area of its main topic and at C1 in other areas it touches; test and fabrication facilities at C1; suppliers that sell or service enabling equipment without developing it at B2; end users exploring quantum technology at B2 in applications. Funders, government and support organisations are listed but not placed on the triangle.

What job ads ask for. Quantum job ads in Denmark are read every Monday from Jobindex. Each ad gets CFQT subdomains from its title and a demanded level from the role: research roles C2, senior and specialist roles C1, engineers and developers B2, technicians and assistants B1, business and other roles A2. Ads from universities are shown apart. The list grows week by week, so the demand builds up over time.

Education and industry. The table compares, per CFQT domain, the courses on the front page, the educators' research, the companies and the job ads. Rows where industry is active but few courses exist point to possible gaps.
