Danish Quantum Education Map

University courses in Denmark mapped to the European Competence Framework for Quantum Technologies (CFQT): which CFQT subdomains each course covers, and which proficiency level it brings students to in each of the three proficiency areas. The mapping is a first estimate from the course descriptions and will be validated with course responsibles through the European Quantum Academy survey.

Coverage of the CFQT content map

Number of courses that cover each subdomain, as primary or secondary content. Empty cells point to gaps in the educational offer. Select a cell to list those courses.

Proficiency levels

Estimated level a course brings students to, per CFQT proficiency area. Courses on enabling technologies such as classical optics or nanofabrication often have no QT-specific proficiency and are counted under "none".

Courses

Method: course lists are harvested from the universities' course catalogues and the national PhD course portal. Each course is assigned up to three CFQT subdomains (the first is primary) and an estimated proficiency level for each area it addresses, based on the course description, study level and workload. The small bars next to each course show how often quantum terms (strong) and related technical terms (weak) occur in its description.

Published by Danish Quantum Academy as part of the mapping task of the European Quantum Academy (Northern European Regional Quantum Academy). Data and code: github.com/danishquantumacademy/kvantekort.
