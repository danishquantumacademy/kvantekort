Quantum Study Path Profiles

What CFQT proficiency profile does a student who completes a programme reach?

How to read this page

The profiles. Choose a study path in the dropdown: dedicated quantum programmes first, then example paths. The triangle is filled up to the highest level a graduate reaches in each proficiency area, based on the quantum-relevant courses in the study path and, if included, a thesis or bachelor project in a quantum topic. The key under the controls explains colours, shades, stripes and the profile outline.

The paths. Dedicated quantum programmes follow their curriculum. The other paths are Danish Quantum Academy's examples of a quantum-focused route through a broader programme; actual students choose their own electives, and programmes also contain non-quantum courses that are not shown. Course roles (compulsory, restricted elective, elective) are taken from the curricula.

Thesis and project. Master's paths assume a thesis in a quantum topic at C1 in area I, the example CFQT gives. Bachelor paths assume a bachelor project in a quantum topic at B2 in area I, with the project size the curriculum gives (10–20 ECTS).

Status. All course levels are first estimates. Method and sources: About the data.
