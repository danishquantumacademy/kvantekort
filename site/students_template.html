Quantum Study Path Profiles

What does the CFQT proficiency triangle look like for a student who completes a programme? Each profile is filled up to the highest level a graduate reaches in each proficiency area, based on the courses in the study path and a master's thesis in a quantum topic. Darker shades mean more courses reach that level. Dedicated quantum programmes follow their curriculum; the other paths are Danish Quantum Academy's examples of a quantum-focused route through a broader programme. All course levels are first estimates.

Programme detail

How profiles are computed: for each proficiency area the profile reaches the highest level among the courses in the path (and the thesis, if included). Band shading counts how many courses reach at least that level. A path lists courses a quantum-focused student would take; actual students choose their own electives, and programmes also contain non-quantum courses that are not shown. Bands reached only through the thesis are striped.

Published by Danish Quantum Academy as part of the mapping task of the European Quantum Academy (Northern European Regional Quantum Academy). Data and code: github.com/danishquantumacademy/kvantekort.
