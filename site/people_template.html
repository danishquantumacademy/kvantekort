Research Behind the Courses

Who teaches the quantum-relevant courses, where they work, what they research, and how that research connects to their courses.

How to read this page

Positions and landscape. The bar shows the position levels of the educators. The research landscape groups them by department, section, group and centre, with the CFQT subdomains their research touches. Open a department to see its groups; unit names link to their own web pages, or to the research portal where a unit has none.

Research proficiency. Each educator's research is placed on the CFQT proficiency triangle, the same triangle as for courses. The evidence is the research profile, the research output since 2021 and the PhD theses the educator supervised (at DTU: ongoing PhD projects as main supervisor). Each piece gets up to three CFQT subdomains; its main topic's area counts at C2 Innovation and other areas it touches at C1 Specialisation. The educator's level in an area is C2 with at least three pieces whose main topic lies there, C1 with one or two (or at least two that touch it) and B2 Investigation with a single one that touches it. The big triangle counts educators per level; select a cell to list them. Each educator row shows the badges for their levels, and the open row shows their own triangle (framed cells = their level) and all evidence.

PhD and postdoc projects. Each project is tagged with up to three CFQT subdomains from its title; the first (filled) is the main topic, which decides the domain in the table. Projects outside the content map, such as catalysis or batteries, are counted separately. Supervised PhD projects come from DTU Orbit, the only portal that shows the main supervisor; for the other universities, PhD students and postdocs are counted through the five research groups.

Educators. One row per educator. Open a row for the research focus, CFQT tags, recent work and courses. The fit note under each course compares the course's CFQT subdomains with the educator's research: Direct fit, Related research, Neighbouring field or Broader base. It describes overlap in subject, not teaching quality; many excellent courses are taught from a neighbouring field.

Status. Positions and units are as stated in the research portals. The research focus and its CFQT tags are written by Danish Quantum Academy and will be checked with the educators. Method and sources: About the data.
