Method (test)

  1. Every piece of evidence (research profile, research output, supervised PhD thesis) gets up to three CFQT subdomains from its title or keywords; the first is the main topic.
  2. Subdomains map to the three proficiency areas as on the rest of the site: (I) Quantum concepts = domains 1–2 and 5.1, 5.4, 5.5; (III) Applications & strategies = 5.6, 6.7, 7.4, 7.6 and domain 8; (II) Hardware & software engineering = all others.
  3. Research creates new knowledge, so the main topic's area is placed at C2 Innovation, and other areas the evidence touches at C1 Specialisation. This fills the cells of the triangle.
  4. The researcher's level per area (framed cells) depends on how much evidence there is: C2 with at least 3 pieces whose main topic is in the area; C1 with at least 1 main topic or at least 2 secondary topics; B2 Investigation with a single secondary topic.
  5. The window is 2021–2026. Older evidence is listed but not counted. Editorials and prefaces are left out.
