Quantum Course Finder

Tell us what you find interesting, in your own words, and get three suggestions for the quantum part of a master's programme (120 ECTS), each with its CFQT proficiency profile. Quantum courses are only part of a master's degree; the rest is your thesis and the other courses in your programme.

Three suggestions

How it works: your text is matched against a list of topics (in English and Danish) that are linked to subdomains of the European Competence Framework for Quantum Technologies (CFQT), and against course titles. Courses are scored by how well their CFQT subdomains match your interests. The first suggestion follows your interests most closely; the other two lean towards hands-on engineering or towards theory, so you can see how the profile changes. Each plan includes a foundation course in quantum concepts if your interests did not already bring one in. The thesis is assumed to reach C1 in (I) Quantum concepts when it is in a quantum topic.

These are suggestions, not study advice. Check your programme's curriculum for which courses count towards your degree and their prerequisites, and talk to your study counsellor. Course levels are first estimates by Danish Quantum Academy.
