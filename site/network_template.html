How quantum researchers in Denmark work together

Every researcher in the 23 quantum research groups is a dot, placed in their group and university. A line joins two researchers who have published together or where one is the other's PhD supervisor. Click a dot to see who that person works with.

How to read this page

The universities are drawn as circles next to their city (the dashed line points to the campus); inside each, the smaller circles are the research groups. Dots in the middle of a group have the most links.

Links come from public sources only: two researchers are linked when the same research output appears on both their university research profiles (newest outputs, read November 2026) or in both their OpenAlex records since 2021, or when DTU Orbit lists one as main supervisor of the other's PhD project. Outputs with more than 30 of the mapped researchers as authors are left out. Thicker lines mean more joint outputs.

The map shows links between groups by default, which is where collaboration across the ecosystem shows. Switch to links between universities, or to all links, including those inside a group. A selected researcher always shows all their links.

The network is incomplete: only the members of the 23 groups are included, and outputs that are not on the profiles or in OpenAlex are missing. A missing line does not mean that two people do not work together.
